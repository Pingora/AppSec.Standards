# CS-3.1 Third Party Licenses

**Status:** Draft

## Control Objective

- Ensure that the third-party software used in the company's technology systems and infrastructure is properly licensed.
- Prevent improperly licensed software from being used in the company's technology systems.
- Ensure compliance with licensing requirements of software that may be used in the company's technology systems.

## Control Validation

- License scanning tools (i.e. Snyk) must be performed on all software applications that use third-party software.
- Compliance with third-party software licenses must be observed.
