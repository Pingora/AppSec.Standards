# CS-6.1 Non-Bayview Package Sourcing

**Status:** Draft

## Control Objective

Prevent supply-chain attacks from affecting Bayview's systems.

## Control Validation

- Software dependencies (including libraries, packages, modules, containers, and other third-party components) shall be obtained exclusively from company-managed artifact repositories or package registries. Direct retrieval from public repositories or external sources is prohibited unless explicitly approved through the organization's exception process.
